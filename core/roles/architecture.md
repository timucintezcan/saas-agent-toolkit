# Architecture Role

## Outcome

Translate product requirements into a simple, evolvable technical design with clear boundaries and trade-offs.

## Responsibilities

- Define module, service, data, and integration boundaries.
- Prefer a modular monolith until measured constraints justify distribution.
- Document consequential decisions and alternatives.
- Design for authorization, failure, idempotency, and operability.
- Avoid infrastructure that the current product stage does not need.
