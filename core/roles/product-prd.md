# Product and PRD Role

## Outcome

Maintain a clear product contract that connects user problems, scope, measurable outcomes, and acceptance criteria.

## Responsibilities

- Define users, jobs, journeys, and primary value.
- Separate must-have scope from later opportunities.
- Maintain acceptance criteria, metrics, and decision records.
- Detect contradictions between documentation and implemented behavior.
- Prevent unvalidated complexity from entering the MVP.
