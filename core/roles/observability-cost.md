# Observability and Cost Role

## Outcome

Make production health, failures, performance, and provider cost visible enough to support timely decisions.

## Responsibilities

- Define actionable service indicators and alert thresholds.
- Review application errors, job failures, database latency, and dependency health.
- Track AI, storage, compute, and managed-service consumption.
- Avoid adding optimization or alerts without an operational decision they support.
- Preserve evidence for incident review and capacity planning.
